# Replay reporting and moderation

Anyone may report a published replay from its replay controls, with or without an account. Reports have a fixed reason and up to 1,000 characters of optional detail. Do not include passwords, provider keys or personal contact details. Reporting does not automatically hide a replay.

Reporting has independent fail-closed budgets of ten notices per trusted client IP per hour and three per replay/IP per day. Repeated notices for a replay from the same IP on the same UTC day are combined without overwriting the original notice. The database stores a purpose-bound HMAC dedup key, not the raw IP. Signed-in reporters must still have a live active account/session. A private, hidden, missing or deletion-pending-owner replay returns the same not-found refusal. Reports contain no copied transcript by default.

Administrators review the bounded queue at `/{lang}/admin/moderation`. Each page contains a bounded set of replay cards and the latest ten reports and ten moderation decisions per card. Hiding, restoring, or keeping a reported replay visible requires a separate, nonempty statement of reasons. Never identify the reporter or copy private report details into that statement. Each decision records its reason, time and administrator in an audit row. The uploader can read the current statement and the latest ten decisions in their own Studio, including after restoration. Reporter identities and other people's reports are excluded from owner/public projections.

Publication and moderation are independent. Republishing cannot clear an administrator's hide. Hiding and restoring do not change an official start, result, score or denominator. Public cards, replay reads, visibility checks and new exports require publication, no hide, and the existing account-deletion retention fence. Finished mounted replays make a lightweight visibility check every 20 seconds and on focus, independently of score polling. Access refusal immediately hides the loaded transcript and cancels/removes its cached query. Temporary errors retain loaded content with retry, but new downloads require a successful fresh check. JSON/PGN downloads and both video encoding/download actions revalidate; encoding workers and generated blob URLs are released when the replay is removed. Already downloaded copies are outside server control. Browser suspension/offline operation can delay scheduled checks until the browser resumes; focus/export checks revalidate then.

Spectator background jobs carry an explicit public visibility generation or exact-owner private purpose. Only hosted games can use server engine work. Admission, execution, each position, conditional write and continuation recheck their current authority. A public job cannot inherit private owner permission, and a hide followed by a restore cannot revive an old public job. Authorized owner-private evaluation remains available while an uploader inspects their own hidden replay. Local/runner replays never trigger server Stockfish.

Deleting a signed-in reporter cascades their report and notes. Deleting an administrator detaches the actor from minimal decision history; deleting a match cascades its reports/actions. Anonymizing a retained published replay preserves its hide, reason and visibility generation. The account export/deletion follow-up must preserve these FK policies and export only reports filed by the requesting user. No new contact address is introduced; the legal pages' existing PENDING contact slot remains for the owner to fill.
