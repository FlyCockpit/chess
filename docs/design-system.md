# Design system

This app follows the FlyCockpit template's design system (`patterns/design-system.md` in the template repo). This file records only where Cockpit Chess differs from it or goes further.

## Edge lines

These are the horizontal edges every container sits on, measured on ink: glyph and icon bounds, or a boxed control's box. Change a value only with the owner's approval, and record the new value here.

Terms: the **text line** is where labels, headings and bare icons start. The **box line** is where filled, bordered or shadowed controls start. The **trailing line** is where the last item in a row ends. An item is within tolerance when its ink is within 1px of its line.

Two rules decide the cases the tables do not list:

- **Boxes share the text line** inside pages, cards, panels and forms. A box does not outdent by its own padding. Ghost icon buttons are centred items, not edge items.
- **Fix a miss in the container**: its padding, a shared inset (`inset`, `--cc-panel-inset`, `px-*` on a track), or the shared component's placement. Never fix one item with a one-off margin.

Five more rules, from the template's back-port of the apps' passes (11-22):

- **An edge text item puts its label on the box's edge.** When a text link or button is the first or last item on a line and its box is wider than its label (a 44px minimum box around a short label), start-align the label for a leading item and end-align it for a trailing one. Centring is for items in centred bars, such as BottomNav, and for icon buttons. The arena nav below follows this.
- **Table text sits on its container's text line.** The first and last cells drop their outer padding (or take the container's inset). Inner cell padding separates columns; it must not indent the first column from the heading above the table. The leaderboard below follows this.
- **Overriding a Button's padding: override every breakpoint.** tailwind-merge treats `px-*` and `md:px-*` as separate groups, so a `className="px-2"` on a Button whose size carries a responsive padding leaves the `md:` value in force. Write both (`px-2 md:px-2`).
- **A control flush with a clipping edge draws its focus ring inside its box** (`focus-visible:ring-inset focus-visible:ring-offset-0`). Button's ring is drawn outside the box, and the viewport or an `overflow-*-hidden` ancestor cuts it off.
- **Frame bands keep the frame line.** Banners above the page (`AdminSetupBanner`) and the header sit 16px inside the frame at every width. They are not re-aligned to a narrower page container below them.

### Signed-in shell (`_app` layouts)

The shell is the template's, and so are its lines: header and page content 16px (`px-4`), default cards 16px, `size="sm"` cards 12px, dropdown menus, the Select listbox, horizontal selector rows and `SegmentedControl`. Differences and additions:

| Container | Text line | Box line | Trailing line | Centre / icon column |

While the first-visit cookie banner shows, both shells pad their bottom by `--consent-clearance` (`apps/web/src/index.css`, from the `--consent-inset` the banner publishes through `components/consent/consent-inset.ts`), so `<main>` ends where the fixed banner starts and the banner never covers page content. The app shell pads by the larger of that and the BottomNav's clearance (`pb-[max(calc(3.5rem_+_var(--safe-area-bottom)),var(--consent-clearance))]`; with the software keyboard up, and from `md:`, by the banner's alone), the arena's `.cc-public-shell` by the larger of that and the home-indicator inset.

Below `sm:` the banner is one short line (`consent:banner.short`) over its two buttons side by side, so a first visit on a phone still shows the page it opened; the title and the full message return from `sm:` up, and the section's `aria-label` carries the title at every width. Below `sm:` the auth card is flat (no surface, ring or inner padding, so the form sits on the page's 16px text line) and starts just under the header instead of being centred in `80vh`; on the login page "Forgot password?" sits in the Password label's row and the sign-up link's 44px box takes half the gap above it. Together they keep the login page's sign-up link visible above the banner without scrolling from 360×740 up (English and Spanish). At 320×568 the signed-out header takes three rows (164px: the brand; Language and theme; Sign Up and Sign In), so the link starts 52px below the banner's top: one short scroll of `<main>` brings it above the banner, and the header's own Sign Up is in view from the start. Re-check that when adding a line to the banner (a cookie-policy link goes inside the short line) or to the login form.

### Arena (`_public` routes and the CLI local lab, `index.css` `.cc-*`)

The arena is its own broadcast surface in plain CSS. Its lines step down at two breakpoints:

| Container | Above 900px | 900px and below | 650px and below |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arena header (`.cc-header`, at most 1440px wide) | Text and trailing lines 40px inside the safe-area frame. The controls group (`ml-auto shrink-0`) never shrinks and ends on the trailing line. | 24px | 16px. The nav wraps to its own row, spread with `space-between`. Brand and controls share the first row while the brand gets its 88px (from 360px in English); below that the controls take a row of their own, still on the trailing line. |
| Header nav (`.cc-nav a`) | Each label starts where its 44px hit box starts (`justify-content: flex-start`), not centred inside it. | Page content (`.cc-app`, at most 1360px wide) | 40px inside the container: headings, hero, section bars, tables, footer. Buttons and panels start on the same line. | 24px | 16px |
